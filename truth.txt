
FPL Graph-RAG Evaluation Questions (Concrete, No Parameters)
===========================================================

Seasons available: 2021-22, 2022-23
Gameweeks: 1–38
All queries are executable directly on Neo4j and align with the KG schema from Create_kg.py

------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. Top 10 players by total points in the 2021-22 season
--------------------------------------------------------
MATCH (s:Season {season_name:'2021-22'})-[:HAS_GW]->(:Gameweek)-[:HAS_FIXTURE]->(f:Fixture)
MATCH (p:Player)-[r:PLAYED_IN]->(f)
WITH p, SUM(r.total_points) AS points
RETURN p.player_name AS player, points
ORDER BY points DESC
LIMIT 10;


Q2. Bottom 10 players by total points in the 2021-22 season (min 900 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
MATCH (s:Season {season_name:'2021-22'})-[:HAS_GW]->(:Gameweek)-[:HAS_FIXTURE]->(f:Fixture)

WITH p, SUM(r.total_points) AS points, SUM(r.minutes) AS minutes

LIMIT 10;

Q11. All fixtures in Gameweek 1 of the 2021-22 season
-----------------------------------------------------
MATCH (s:Season {season_name:'2021-22'})-[:HAS_GW]->(gw:Gameweek {GW_number:1})-[:HAS_FIXTURE]->(f:Fixture)
MATCH (f)-[:HAS_HOME_TEAM]->(home:Team)
MATCH (f)-[:HAS_AWAY_TEAM]->(away:Team)
RETURN f.fixture_number AS fixture, home.name AS home, away.name AS away, f.kickoff_time
ORDER BY fixture;
